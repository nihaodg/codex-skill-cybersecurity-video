# Workflow Checklist

## Phase 1

- Confirm topic, audience, runtime, ratio, and delivery format.
- Decide whether the task is preview-first or final-video-first.
- Pick a visual direction before writing too much code.

## Phase 2

- Build the HTML prototype or adapt the existing project.
- Make the first scene the strongest scene.
- Keep typography, contrast, and spacing presentation-grade.

## Phase 3

- Write scenes before writing final narration.
- Split narration by scene.
- Reuse the same scene text as the subtitle source.

## Phase 4

- Generate per-scene voiceover.
- Measure real audio durations.
- Add lead-in and tail buffers per scene.
- Keep music quieter than narration.

## Phase 5

- Export preview frames.
- Export at least one preview video.
- Fix sync, pacing, subtitle density, and layout repetition.

## Final Delivery

- Confirm final MP4 exists.
- Confirm duration is reasonable.
- Confirm audio and video streams are present.
- Summarize any known tradeoffs or remaining risks.
